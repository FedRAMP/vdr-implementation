# Agency-Provided Offerings

Cloud service offerings that are provided by federal agencies are already subject to the requirements in CISA BOD 26-04 directly and are therefore exempt from the VDR/VER deadlines as long as they intend to transition to FedRAMP A-130 Class G Certification.

In the FedRAMP Marketplace such offerings are currently referred to as "**By Government, For Government**."

!!! warning "Agency-Provided Offering guidance is forthcoming!"

    As of 2026-11-03, formal information on Agency-Provided Offerings has not been published publicly. This Certification Type will
    be added during FedRAMP 20x Phase 4 and piloted with agencies as a way for agency-provided offerings to obtain a FedRAMP Certification
    following the requirements in the OMB A-130 instead of commercial-specific requirements from FedRAMP.

## Scope of Agency-Provided Offerings

!!! info "Draft Information"

    For awareness, FedRAMP is including the following information from the upcoming guidance on Agency-Provided Offerings.

To be eligible for a FedRAMP Class G Certification, agency-provided cloud service offerings must meet ALL of the following requirements:

1. The cloud service offering is within the scope of FedRAMP (especially, it is a shared service intended to be used by multiple agencies).
2. The cloud service offering is operated by an agency (including under direct contract where legally relevant).
3. The authorizing official, system owner, privacy official, and all other key RMF roles are filled by appropriate federal officials or contractors operating under authority and responsibility delegated from federal officials.

FedRAMP recommends that system owners unsure if their cloud service is within the scope of Offerings by Agency consult with appropriate counsel.
