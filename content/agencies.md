FedRAMP determines the level of risk appropriate for a FedRAMP Certification.

Agencies determine the level of risk appropriate to their specific use cases of a cloud service.

This allows FedRAMP to enable some flexibility in the deadlines for implementing the requirements in BOD 26-04 via FedRAMP’s VDR/VER ruleset.

Some agencies will accept the risk of this flexibility. Others will not.

Do not decide your investment priority based solely on maintaining FedRAMP Certification.

Some agencies may stop using your cloud service offering in December if you have not implemented the VDR/VER, because they are unwilling to accept the risk.


## Agency Customer Experience

Always consider the customer experience of your agency customers. FedRAMP is deciding what is good enough for a general government-wide use case, but some of your agencies will approach this differently.

Never be like “well FedRAMP said this was all we had to do” if an agency asks for more information - consider the customer experience. Show off how seriously you’re taking this.
